Fixes a crash that left a grey box on the reading-stats screen.

Library
- The reading-stats screen no longer shows a grey box where the "Most-Read
  Series" card should be. One of your top series having a shortened link — which
  happens with entries brought over from another app — crashed that card, and a
  crash renders as a blank grey box rather than an error. The same faulty
  handling was fixed in one other place, so a source with an unusual address
  can't trip it either.
