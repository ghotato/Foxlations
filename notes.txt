Foxlations v1.0.0 — the first official release

Foxlations now runs on-device extensions for manga, manhwa, manhua, light novels,
and anime — no computer or server needed. Add a source, and read or watch right on
your device.

Highlights
- Anime streaming — add an anime source and watch episodes in the built-in player;
  the app handles the extension on-device and finds the video for you.
- On-device extensions — a wide range of existing source formats work directly,
  no external tooling required.
- Continue where you left off — reopen an episode to pick up right where you
  stopped watching, and light novels return you to your exact scroll position.
- Gets past Cloudflare — protected sources clear the check with a quick in-app step,
  and it's remembered.
- Volume-button reading — tap to turn a page or scroll a screen, hold to scroll
  fluidly at a speed you set (Android; experimental on iOS).
- iOS, Android, and Windows.

Also in this release
- Player: fixed seek-bar clipping in landscape, resume that actually sticks,
  scrollable subtitle/audio/quality menus, and separate-audio-track playback.
- Reading: light-novel chapters and ID-keyed pages load; installed sources survive
  app re-signs; large source repos show correct language and content rating.
- Stability: no more crash when reopening from the background (iOS); every error
  surfaces in More > Debug logs; nothing hides under the Android navigation bar.

Full change list is in the app: More > About > What's New.
