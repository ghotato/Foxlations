v0.2.0 — security & encryption, RepoForge framework coverage, and a batch of
library/UX work. The largest release so far, and the one hardened for going
public.

Security & privacy
- Your login tokens for AniList / MyAnimeList / Kitsu are now encrypted with a
  key held in the device keystore, instead of sitting in plain storage where
  anything with file access could read them and impersonate you.
- The vault is now genuinely encrypted (PBKDF2 + AES) with your password, not
  just hidden. Its contents stay locked until you unlock, and leaving the vault
  drops the decrypted copy from memory.
  (One-time: reconnect trackers once, and re-enter your vault password so it
  re-encrypts under the new scheme.)
- A full security review before open-sourcing: connections now verify their
  certificates, extensions can no longer be tricked into running hidden code
  via a crafted source name or URL, sources must load over https, and cookies
  no longer leak across sibling sites.

RepoForge
- Recognises 60+ more site frameworks across manga, anime AND light novels
  (Madara, MangaThemesia, foolslide, GigaViewer, the major novel CMSes, and
  many more). When it detects one, it auto-fills the selector slots with what
  that framework uses.
- Scraping Studio: every selector field now has a searchable list of known
  selectors to pick from, each showing a live count of how many elements it
  matches — so you don't have to know CSS. The test button is clearer, and
  "0 matches" reads as a gentle "no match" rather than a red error.
- No longer mislabels sites as an "AniList API" just because they link to
  AniList; sites are identified by their real framework.

Library & sources
- Light Novel tab added to Sources and Extensions (novel sources were parsed
  but had nowhere to show).
- Pull the library down to update whatever category you're currently viewing.
- Empty results now say the right thing — "No anime found" / "No light novels
  found" instead of always "No manga".
- The vault's secret opener moved to the Foxlations logo on the About page
  (tap it 9×), and you can now move individual titles — with their read
  progress — in and out of the vault by type.

Readability & size
- Text across the whole app is easier to read: subtitles, captions,
  placeholders and dividers were too faint; button labels could be white on a
  light accent; toasts blended into the page. All fixed.
- The Android APK is roughly half the size — it was shipping CPU architectures
  (32-bit ARM, x86) it never needed.

Other
- "Check for Updates" actually checks now and tells you what changed; Android
  offers the new APK directly, iPhone points at AltStore.

Known gaps
- Some of the newly-added framework generators (the JSON-API ones) are
  best-effort and may need tuning against specific sites.
- iOS text recognition for Japanese/Korean/Chinese is included but unverified;
  CI checks whether the models actually linked.
