The Manga / Anime / Light Novel libraries now filter properly, translation is
better on-device, and there's a new free OpenRouter option.

Libraries
- The separate Manga / Anime / Light Novel libraries now actually filter —
  before, picking Anime still showed manga. Long-press the Library tab and a
  small bubble pops up above it: All (everything — the default), Manga, Anime or
  Light Novels. Picking one shows just that kind, and your choice sticks after
  you leave or close the app. The Library tab icon, the "Add" button (Add Manga
  / Add Anime / Add LNs) and the Stats page all follow the current library.
- Swipe left or right anywhere in the library grid to slide to the next or
  previous category — no need to reach up and tap the category tabs.
- The number badge on each cover now counts UNREAD chapters and ticks down as
  you read, instead of showing a fixed total that never changed.

Translation
- Translated text now sits ON the page, over the speech bubbles it belongs to,
  instead of floating in a fixed layer. It rides along with each image, so it
  stays put when you zoom or pan.
- Translate-as-you-scroll: turn on AI Translate and keep reading — new pages are
  translated automatically as they scroll into view (great for long webtoons),
  not just the one page you started on. Tap any bubble to flip between the
  translation and the original text.
- The AI Translate button now only shows when you've turned AI translation on in
  Settings > AI. Before, it could appear (and run) even with the feature off.
- Better on-device translation: it now auto-detects the page's language (Korean,
  Japanese, Chinese or Latin) instead of assuming Japanese, so text is read and
  translated correctly rather than coming out as garbled fragments — and it
  ignores stray "text" picked up over the artwork.
- New OpenRouter provider, with free models available (needs a free OpenRouter
  API key). It joins the on-device (offline) and Google Translate free options.
  A local Koharu server still gives the highest-quality results.
