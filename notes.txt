RepoForge recognises more site layouts, so generated sources pick up the right
things on more sites.

RepoForge
- Five more site layouts are now understood properly: MadTheme, WPComics,
  ZeistManga, FMReader and nhentai-style galleries. These were already being
  identified by name, but nothing was known about how they arrange their pages,
  so a generated source fell back to generic guesses — usually finding nothing,
  and occasionally picking up an advert instead of a title. Each now has its own
  list, title, cover, chapter and page layouts.
- Search now works in generated sources for sites whose results page is laid out
  differently from their browse page. Only the browse layout was recognised, so
  a source could list titles perfectly and then find nothing at all for every
  search.
